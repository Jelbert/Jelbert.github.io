Salo Ulan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Salo Ulan game ("the game") handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is developed by Jelbert ("we").

Summary

  • No sign-up, no email, no real name, no contacts, no location.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases. Piso and items are earned only by playing.
  • To run the online ranking, the game creates an anonymous player account and stores your ranking name, best score, round reached and date.
  • Your ranking name, best score, round and date are shown publicly on the in-game ranking.
  • On Chismisan, the in-game bulletin board (ages 13 and up), you can post short messages and comment on other players' posts. Your posts and comments are shown publicly to other players, with your ranking name.
  • You can turn the online ranking off, or delete your online data, at any time in Settings.
  • Everything else (piso, items, medals, settings) stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file on your device with your piso, the items you own and use, your round and best score, your medals and daily goals, your settings (sound and volumes, online ranking on or off), your ranking name, and the sign-in token of your anonymous player account. It never leaves your device except as described below. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Information stored online

The online ranking is on when you first open the game. While it is on and your device is online, the game signs in to our ranking service as an anonymous player. We then store:

Please don't use your real name or other personal details as your ranking name. Names are filtered for offensive words, and we may rename or remove entries that break this policy.

The ranking runs on Supabase (supabase.com), which hosts the database for us as a service provider. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not sell or share this information with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.

Chismisan (the bulletin board)

  • Ages 13 and up. The first time you open the board, the game asks your year of birth. It does not store or send the year: your device only remembers whether the board is open to you, and the answer can't be changed afterwards.
  • Rules first. Before you can read or post, you accept the board's rules: no swearing or sexual words, no bullying, threats or toxic talk, no racism, no politics or government talk, no real names, numbers, addresses, social media or links of anyone. Posts and comments that mention politics or government, contain slurs, threats or swearing, or include links or phone numbers are refused automatically.
  • Filtering and moderation. Posts are checked automatically, and links, phone numbers and e-mail addresses are refused. Anyone can report a post or comment; one reported by several players is hidden until we review it. We review reports and remove posts or ban accounts that break the rules.
  • Your controls. You can delete your own posts and comments at any time (and comments under your own posts), and block a player so you no longer see their posts and comments.
  • Cost. A post costs 100 piso and a comment 50 piso, the game's own coins earned by playing. No real money is involved.

Retention and deletion

  • Ranking entries are kept while the ranking runs. We never delete accounts automatically.
  • Chismisan posts and comments are kept until you delete them, you delete your online data, or we remove them for breaking the rules.
  • To delete your online data at any time, open Settings → Online data → BURAHIN in the game and confirm. This immediately deletes your ranking entry, your Chismisan posts, comments, reports and blocks, and your anonymous player account, and switches the online ranking off on your device. Your piso, items, medals and best score stay on the device; you can rejoin later as a new player.
  • You can also email jelbertnixonbasco@gmail.com with your ranking name (and roughly when you set your best score) and we'll delete your entry and account within 30 days.
  • You can switch the online ranking off at any time in Settings → Online ranking. While it's off, the game makes no network connections.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Chismisan is closed to players who say they are under 13. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a ranking name, in a post or in a comment, contact us and we'll remove it.
